Understanding Tooth Decay

1

Demineralization

Earliest stage where minerals begin to weaken enamel and white spots may appear.

2

Enamel Decay

Bacteria break down enamel creating visible defects, discoloration or small holes.

3

Dentin Decay

Decay reaches dentin causing sensitivity and progression toward deeper tissues.

4

Pulp Damage

Severe decay reaching the pulp — pain, infection and urgent treatment required.

Prevention Tips
  • Brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste
  • Floss daily to remove plaque
  • Limit sugary foods and drinks
  • Visit your dentist regularly
